### 📚 Topic
- Model Context Protocol (MCP)

### 🧠 What I Learned
MCP is not another AI. It's a communication protocol that lets AI applications use external tools through a standardized interface.

### 💡 The "Aha!" Moment 
I finally understand why everyone compares MCP to USB. The important part isn't the protocol itself — it's that every AI application only needs to learn one way to communicate with thousands of tools.
